# OpenMoji artwork

The black SVG files in `openmoji-black/` are unmodified artwork from [OpenMoji](https://openmoji.org/), created by the OpenMoji contributors. They are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

The 126 included entries are all items in OpenMoji's [`data/openmoji.json`](https://github.com/hfg-gmuend/openmoji/blob/aeb8bb3a59e2de39c754ac79180c8131c906acea/data/openmoji.json) at revision `aeb8bb3a59e2de39c754ac79180c8131c906acea` whose English `annotation` contains the whole word “face” (case insensitive). Their codes and names are recorded in [`faces.lua`](faces.lua). This literal name filter includes animal and celestial faces as well as facial expressions.

Source files: [OpenMoji black SVGs](https://github.com/hfg-gmuend/openmoji/tree/aeb8bb3a59e2de39c754ac79180c8131c906acea/black/svg). No changes were made to the SVG artwork.
